Why OctaSpace can win

Three structural advantages the centralized incumbents can't copy.

⛓️

Truly decentralized

OctaSpace runs on its own Layer-1 blockchain. No single company sets fees, bans regions, or takes a cut of every rental — settlement is on-chain with escrow and provider reputation built into the protocol.

💸

Providers keep more

You set your own rental prices and get paid in OCTA per minute of use. And when nobody's renting, idle-mining kicks in automatically — unrented time still earns instead of sitting idle.

🧰

More than GPUs

One network for GPU compute, AI/ML environments, distributed rendering, IPFS storage, and VPN exit nodes. Renters get a full decentralized cloud; providers monetize every spare resource.
