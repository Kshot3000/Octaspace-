What OctaSpace must fix to win

Our honest punch-list. We're working the code side too.

🔓

Make staking accessible

Staking is optional — but the 100k OCTA collateral (… at live prices, per the whitepaper) concentrates staking rewards among large holders. Tiered staking or delegated pools would widen participation in the reward stream.

💵

Fiat off-ramps

Salad pays PayPal. OctaSpace pays OCTA into thin liquidity. A built-in swap-to-stable path keeps providers from dreading payout day.

📈

Demand-side liquidity

Marketplaces die without renters. Price aggregators, renter UX polish, and the comparison content on this site exist to fix exactly this.

🪟

One-click onboarding

Windows node support is in beta — Salad's whole empire is built on one-click Windows installs. Finish it, polish it, shout about it.
